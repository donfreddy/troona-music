# Troona privacy policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026
Last updated: November 22, 2026

Troona plays the music stored on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no analytics.

## On this device

Your library, likes, playlists and listening history are stored only on this phone. Troona reads your audio files and never your photos, videos or documents.

## Online extras (off by default)

There are two separate switches. Bios sends artist and album names to Last.fm. Photos sends them to Deezer, with up to three titles of each artist's songs (their most played) so that the photo found is of that artist and not a namesake, and downloads pictures from Deezer or Last.fm. These services also see your IP address, as with any web request. No file, account or play count is sent. Turn either switch off at any time in Settings: requests to that service stop, and turning photos off also deletes the photos already downloaded. "Clear downloaded info" deletes everything fetched online.

- Last.fm privacy policy: https://www.last.fm/legal/privacy
- Deezer privacy policy: https://www.deezer.com/legal/personal-datas

## Lyrics search (off by default)

When on, opening the lyrics of a song that has none stored sends its title, artist, album and length to LRCLIB, then, if it has none, its artist and title to lyrics.ovh. Nothing is sent for other songs, and never for your whole library. These services also see your IP address, as with any web request. Lyrics found are kept on this phone; "Clear downloaded info" deletes them. Lyrics you import from a file never leave this phone, except in a backup you export.

- LRCLIB: https://lrclib.net
- lyrics.ovh: https://lyrics.ovh

## Update check (off by default)

When on, Troona asks GitHub once a day for the number of its latest version. "Check for updates now" asks once, only when you tap it. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request; no data about you or your music is sent. Troona never downloads or installs an update itself: "View" opens the release page in your browser.

- GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## Problem reports

Errors are written to a log on this device. It can contain file names. Nothing is sent unless you choose Report a problem and share it yourself.

## Backups

A backup holds the parts you choose (playlists, likes, listening history, settings, equalizer), with the names and sizes of your music files, unencrypted. Troona writes it to the folder you picked for backups, or hands it to the app you choose when you share it. It goes nowhere else. Restoring reads only the backup you pick, and writes only the parts you keep.
Exporting a backup writes your playlists, likes, listening history, imported lyrics, the timing you set on lyrics and settings, with the names and sizes of your music files, to a file, unencrypted, and hands it to the app you choose. It goes nowhere else. Restoring reads only the file you pick.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Troona deletes everything it stored. Your music files, and backups in your backup folder or exported, are never touched.
